How to Explain This Queue Project

1. The Core Concept (FIFO): Just like cars arriving at a restaurant drive-thru, the first car to join the line (`enqueue`) is the first car served at the pickup window (`dequeue`).

2. Two-Step Window Lifecycle:

  • Serving Now: The car reaches the window and food preparation/handover is occurring.
  • Served (Driver Leaves): Food is delivered, driver leaves, total count increments, and the order is logged in the transaction history ledger with receipt options.

3. POS & Quantity Selection: Features inline menu drop-downs to select exact quantities before items are bundled into the final order string.
